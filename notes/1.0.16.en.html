What's new in Atelier 1.0.16

  • At the top of the window, clearly visible: “Undo” and “Redo” to step back one change at a time (the step’s name shows in the tooltip), and a blue “Save” button, in Essentials, Studio and the Library. Edits are still kept automatically in Atelier; Save writes the edited photo to a file (a copy next to the original or a replacement), like ⌘S.
  • Fix: the Library thumbnails and preview of a photo straightened then cropped showed empty edges. They now use the crop, and those already affected are redone automatically at launch.
  • Fix: a photo straightened then cropped in Essentials reappeared with empty edges when reopened. The crop was saved, but the Studio’s Crop tool, restored on opening, showed the uncropped photo; it no longer does outside the Studio.
