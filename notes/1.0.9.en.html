What's new in Atelier 1.0.9

  • Studio, inspired by Photoshop: quick mask (Q) to paint a selection in red; rulers (⌘R) and snapping guides; Navigator panel (⌥⌘6); snapshots in Step-by-step Undo; Blend If in layer options; Pen tool for Bézier paths (selection, fill, stroke, shape layer); smart objects (scale without loss) and Free Transform (⌘T); floating contextual bar under the selection; Properties panel that follows the active layer (⌥⌘7).
