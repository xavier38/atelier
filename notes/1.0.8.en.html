What's new in Atelier 1.0.8

  • Studio: new tool bar with colourful icons in two columns, grouped by family (selection in blue, painting in orange, retouching in green, transform in purple, text and shapes in pink). On hover, a bubble explains each tool: name, shortcut, what it does and how to use it. Primary and secondary colours sit just below. The button at the top brings back the detailed list.
  • Library: deleting photos or a folder now updates the folder tree and the counts right away. Only the affected folder is recounted, the other counts stay on screen, and a deleted folder’s row disappears immediately without rebuilding the whole tree.
