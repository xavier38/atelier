What's new in Atelier 0.3.17

  • New: File > Convert Images… (and right-click in the Photo library). Converts images or whole folders at once to JPEG, PNG, HEIC, AVIF, TIFF, GIF, BMP, ICO and ICNS icons, JPEG 2000, TGA, PSD or PDF, with presets (Instagram, email, half size…), longest side, maximum file size and EXIF removal. Originals are never changed.
