What's new in Atelier 1.0.2

  • Library: new “My Shortcuts” section at the top of the column. Add any folder with the + button, by dragging it from the Finder or with right-click → Add to Shortcuts; shortcuts expand like the folder tree. New ↻ Refresh button: reads the whole folder list again (including OneDrive or a drive plugged in meanwhile) and the photos, keeping open branches open.
