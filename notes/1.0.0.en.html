What's new in Atelier 1.0.0

  • Atelier reaches 1.0 after a large audit (memory, CPU, data safety). It holds less memory (no more leaks after the histogram, effects, photo merge or analysis; a large photo fits in less space), responds better (lighter hovering and Refine slider, faster launch), and autosave protects your edits better: it works in the background, reports a failure and offers to retry, and “Revert to original” no longer brings them back. Only one instance of Atelier runs at a time.
