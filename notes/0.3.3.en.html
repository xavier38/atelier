What's new in Atelier 0.3.3

  • Three clearly visible modules in the centre of the toolbar: Photo Library, Essential and Studio, each with its own icon. The toolbar adapts to the window width.
  • Photo Library: browse folders and authorised Apple Photos, view uncropped thumbnails with portrait framing and orientation preserved, use corrected multiple selection and recursive photo counts.
  • Folders: create, rename, move to Trash and move files or folders. Drag and drop clearly highlights the destination; collisions and prohibited destinations are refused. Menu commands also let you move items without dragging.
  • Essential has a refreshed layout for guided adjustments. A warning explains when an image is missing; Studio remains available to create a blank image.
  • Studio: section headings are easier to distinguish from their controls, and several sections can stay expanded. Adjacent and global colour selection are corrected; layers, masks, adjustments, editable filters, editable text and transformations share the same workspace.
  • High precision: 16/32-bit TIFF and RAW formats recognised by macOS, linear Float32 processing, colour profiles, editable adjustments, layers, floating-point masks and 29 blend modes. Projects retain the RAW original and its rendering. Export 8/16-bit TIFF or linear Float32 TIFF; legacy tools requiring 8 bits use an explicit copy.
  • Metadata: a detailed panel shows properties recognised by macOS, including capture date, camera, lens, GPS and author. Projects and compatible exports retain metadata; export dimensions and orientation are updated. Opaque manufacturer data remains stored in the project.
  • Responsiveness and readability: lighter folder browsing, edit lookup and selection processing, with better controlled selection outlines. Improved keyboard navigation, visible focus, contrast and button sizes.
  • Reliability: fixes for window crashes and 256-colour palettes in indexed PNG exports, repaired LaMa download buttons, and stronger checks for damaged files and overly complex metadata.
