What's new in Atelier 1.0.10

  • Studio: grouped tools like Photoshop. One icon per family (Brush / Pencil / Recolour, Healing / Patch / Remove object…) with a small triangle; a long click or right-click shows the variants, and the icon keeps the last tool chosen. The bubble lists the variants; ⇧ + the key moves to the next one. Photo, Painting and Create workspaces (View menu) remember which panels are open.
