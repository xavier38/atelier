What's new in Atelier 1.0.13

  • Library: Atelier remembers the expanded folders in each section, subfolders included, and the column’s scroll position; on return the structure is re-read and folders that disappeared are forgotten.
  • Studio: clearer tool bar, with a name under each icon, a coloured title for each family (Select, Paint, Fix…) and lighter colours; the “List” and “Space: …” buttons are spelled out.
