What's new in Atelier 0.3.23

  • Atelier holds less memory and responds better: no more leaks after the histogram, effects, photo merging and analysis; erase models are released; an already analysed photo is no longer re-analysed at every launch; zone hovering and the Refine slider are lighter; autosave runs in the background without stutter; the Photo Library opens faster (albums and folders loaded in the background).
