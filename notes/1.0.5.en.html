What's new in Atelier 1.0.5

  • A big update. Library: search by content (“dog”, “beach”, “sunset”… analysed on your Mac), stars, colour labels and keywords (keys 0–9, kept in an XMP file next to the photo, readable by Lightroom), and “Find Duplicates…” to keep the best shot of a burst. Studio: shapes stay editable (rectangle, ellipse, star, arrow, line… with colour, outline, corners, gradient) and “Rasterize Layer”. Photoshop: PSD files open with their layers, groups and masks, and Atelier saves PSD with layers. Memory: a single-layer photo uses about 100 MB less, and autosave after a brush stroke drops from several seconds to a few hundredths. Fix: access to Apple Photos and to the camera was refused by macOS.
