What's new in Atelier 1.0.11

  • Library: new “Online services” section with OneDrive, Google Drive, Dropbox, Box, pCloud and MEGA, found automatically. In Settings, show or hide each service and choose its photo folder. Each section of the left column (My shortcuts, Folders, Online services, Apple Photos, Atelier) collapses and expands with its chevron, and Atelier remembers it.
  • Fix: Atelier could refuse to start with the message “The file com.xavierroubaud.atelier couldn’t be saved in the folder Application Support”. An empty old data folder is now removed, and launching is never blocked.
